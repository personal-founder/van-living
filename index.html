Ground heights & structure

Ground is Y = 0 and the tires touch that plane. Published reference: passenger floor surface 44 in; first step surface 15.5 in; intermediate riser and tread 9.75 in; step width 35.75 in. Tread surfaces are 15.5, 25.25 and 35 in with the default dimensions; the final rise is 9 in to meet the 44-in floor. The brochure figures do not form identical risers all the way to the floor; this last rise is derived, not verified.

Cab floor 34 in, front bumper bottom 21 in and rear bumper bottom 24 in remain editable estimates.
